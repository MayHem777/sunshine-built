## The brief

The owners had a large steel shed on their block in Ninderry and wanted to live in it. That meant turning a Class 10 shed into a Class 1 home: two bedrooms, a living area, kitchen, bathroom and laundry, plus a new extension and a covered terrace out the back.

## What we did

**The shed.** We stripped the old wall cladding, windows and internal racking back to the steel frame. The roof sheets came off a few at a time so an insulation blanket could go underneath, then went back on with new screws and flashings. The walls were re-clad in new metal cladding over wall wrap with a thermal break.

**Slab, frame and services.** New slab and footings, with a step-down for the wet areas. New timber wall and ceiling frames inside the shed, with insulation in the external walls, internal walls and roof. Plumbing, gas, septic and electrical were done by licensed trades and signed off.

**The extension and terrace.** A new extension under its own metal roof, and a covered terrace with an insulated panel roof on hardwood posts and beams.

**Inside.** Aluminium windows and doors with insect screens, plasterboard walls, plywood ceilings, hybrid timber-look floors and cavity sliding doors. Custom cabinetry in the kitchen, bathroom and laundry. The bathroom was waterproofed to the Australian Standard and tiled floor to ceiling in the shower.

## Approvals and sign-off

The change of use had engineering sign-off, and we finished with the plumbing final certificate, an approved as-constructed drainage plan and compliance certificates for the termite barrier, insulation, and window and door installation.
