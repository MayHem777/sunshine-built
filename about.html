Our Story

Built on Experience. Run on Reputation.

Sunshine Built has been operating across Noosa and the Sunshine Coast for over 20 years. We started as a carpentry and renovation outfit and grew into a full-service licensed building company — new homes, commercial fit-outs, insurance repairs, custom saunas, and more.

What hasn't changed is how we work. We're a small, hands-on team. When you hire Sunshine Built, you're dealing directly with the builder — not a project manager, not an office admin, not a subcontractor who's never met you. You get the builder on the phone, on site, and accountable for the result.

Our work has built a reputation across the Noosa region — mostly through word of mouth, which is how we prefer it. If the build is right, the referrals come naturally.

Our Principles

How We Approach Every Job

These aren't marketing lines. They're the things that have kept our clients calling back for 20 years.

Quality Over Speed

We work to a standard, not a deadline. That said — we're efficient. We just don't let efficiency become a reason to cut corners on the finish.

No Surprises

If something changes mid-build — material costs, structural discoveries, timeline shifts — you'll know about it before it becomes a problem. Straight talk, always.

Tidy Sites

We leave a clean worksite every day and a spotless property on handover. You shouldn't have to do a thing after we leave except enjoy the result.

Fair Pricing

We're not the cheapest and we don't try to be. But we're competitive, and you'll see exactly where every dollar goes. No hidden margins, no mates rates in reverse.
