Our Story

We started as a carpentry outfit. Twenty years later, we build everything — and we still care about the details.

Sunshine Built began with a focus on carpentry and renovation work. Over two decades we've grown into a full-service licensed building company — new homes, commercial fit-outs, insurance repairs, custom saunas, and more.

What hasn't changed is how we work. We're a small, hands-on team. When you hire Sunshine Built, you're dealing directly with the builder — not a project manager who has never picked up a tool. You get the builder on the phone, on site, and accountable for the result.

Our reputation across the Noosa region has been built almost entirely on word of mouth. We think that says more than any award or accreditation.

"Every client deserves a builder who genuinely cares about the outcome — not just getting through the job."
How We Work
  • Quality Over Speed We work to a standard, not a deadline. That said — we're efficient. We just don't let efficiency become an excuse to cut corners on the finish.
  • No Surprises If something changes mid-build — material costs, structural discoveries, timeline shifts — you'll know about it before it becomes a problem. Straight talk, always.
  • Tidy Sites We leave a clean worksite every day and a spotless property on handover. You shouldn't have to do a thing after we leave except enjoy the result.
  • Fair Pricing We're not the cheapest and we don't try to be. But we're competitive, and you'll see exactly where every dollar goes. No hidden margins, no overpriced variations.
