About Sunshine Built

Local Builders Who Take Pride in the Details.

Sunshine Built was built on a simple belief — that every client deserves a builder who genuinely cares about the outcome. With over 20 years of hands-on experience across the Noosa region, we've built a reputation for delivering quality work, on time, with honest communication throughout.

We're a small, tight team. That means you're dealing directly with the builder — not a project manager who's never picked up a tool.
